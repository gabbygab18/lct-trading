## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog is an open shadow-foam tool case: each SKU nests in its own cut pocket with a stamped steel plate; what you pull into the cart and what is out of stock both show the red under-layer. Refuses the white e-commerce card grid with banner carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal closed-cell foam ground (#1b1b1d / #232326) with pocket cuts as inset shadows, signal red (#d7141a) only as the under-layer and primary action, brushed-steel plates (#c7c9cc→#9ea1a5) carrying SKU and price in a condensed stamped face, hex-nut geometry from the logo for badges and qty. Light mode: none; foam is the ground. Dense rows in list view at one type size, rank by weight/case/reversal.

STORY: Visitor sees the whole case immediately, types a name or SKU, sets qty on the pocket, adds; the tray counts up. Tray opens to a slip, then a checkout mirroring the familiar PH form (contact, ship/pickup, barangay address, courier preference, COD/bank deposit), ends with a stamped reference number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rail: logo hex + wordmark left, full-width search (name/SKU) center, tray button with count right. Brand rail of 14 stamped tabs below. Then the pocket grid starts in the first viewport (4-5 cols desktop, 2 cols mobile), no hero banner. Primary action: Add on each pocket plate.

FORM: Shadow-foam tool case, candidate 6 of 7 on my list, seed key 5fdbad8c. Raises: single-size SKU list view (timetable); designed empty-pocket out-of-stock (seven-segment); focus-only controls lift (streaming). Signature interaction: adding drops the item out of its pocket to the tray and the pocket flashes red under-layer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
